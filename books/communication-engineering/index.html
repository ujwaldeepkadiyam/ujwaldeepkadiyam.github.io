Communication Engineering

Books on signals, communication systems, wireless technologies, and signal-processing methods.

Signals and Systems

Signals and Systems

Alan V. Oppenheim, Alan S. Willsky, and S. Hamid Nawab

2nd edition, 1997

This foundational text develops continuous-time and discrete-time signals, linear time-invariant systems, Fourier analysis, Laplace transforms, and z-transforms in a unified framework. Its mathematical treatment supports advanced work in communications, control, signal processing, and system theory.

Recommended

Linear Systems and Signals

B. P. Lathi and Roger A. Green

3rd edition, 2018

Lathi and Green cover linear systems, convolution, Fourier methods, sampling, modulation, Laplace transforms, state-space analysis, and discrete-time systems. The combination of mathematical development and engineering examples prepares students for communications, control, and signal processing.

Recommended

Signals and Systems

Simon Haykin and Barry Van Veen

2nd edition, 2002

Haykin and Van Veen present continuous-time and discrete-time analysis with system representations, Fourier transforms, filtering, sampling, modulation, and feedback. Its balance of theory and applications supports engineering study and preparation for specialized communication and DSP work.

Recommended

Digital and Wireless Communications

Digital Communications

John G. Proakis and Masoud Salehi

5th edition, 2008

This graduate classic develops signal-space methods, digital modulation, optimum receivers, channel models, error-control coding, equalization, spread spectrum, fading, and multiple-antenna communication. Its analytical depth makes it central to coding, detection, and digital communication research.

Recommended

Wireless Communications

Andrea Goldsmith

2nd edition, forthcoming December 2026

Goldsmith develops wireless-system theory and design through propagation, fading, capacity, modulation, diversity, multiple antennas, multiuser communication, and cellular networks. The revised edition adds modern technologies and is intended for advanced students and wireless researchers.

Forthcoming

Fundamentals of Wireless Communication

David Tse and Pramod Viswanath

1st edition, 2005

Tse and Viswanath unify fading channels, diversity, capacity, multiple access, interference, MIMO, space-time communication, and opportunistic scheduling. The text connects information-theoretic limits with practical wireless-system design.

Recommended

Signal Processing

Discrete-Time Signal Processing

Alan V. Oppenheim and Ronald W. Schafer

3rd edition, 2010

This authoritative text develops discrete-time systems, Fourier and z-transform analysis, sampling, filter structures, filter design, multirate processing, and spectral techniques. It directly supports digital communications and implementation-oriented DSP research.

Recommended

Digital Signal Processing: Principles, Algorithms, and Applications

John G. Proakis and Dimitris G. Manolakis

4th edition, 2007

Proakis and Manolakis cover discrete-time systems, transform-domain analysis, digital-filter design, spectral estimation, multirate processing, and practical DSP algorithms. It is especially relevant to communication receivers, speech processing, and computational signal analysis.

Recommended

Adaptive Filter Theory

Simon Haykin

5th edition, 2014

Haykin develops adaptive filtering through Wiener filters, least-mean-square algorithms, recursive least squares, Kalman filtering, and related learning methods. It is an advanced reference for channel equalization, echo cancellation, array processing, and estimation.

Recommended
