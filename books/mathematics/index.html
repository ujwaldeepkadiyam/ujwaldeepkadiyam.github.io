Mathematics

Mathematical foundations for communication engineering, coding theory, theoretical computer science, and data science.

Linear Algebra and Matrix Theory Probability and Random Processes Discrete Mathematics Abstract Algebra and Finite Fields Information Theory Optimization Graph Theory and Networks Numerical Methods Statistical Mathematics Mathematical Foundations

Linear Algebra and Matrix Theory

Linear Algebra Done Right

Sheldon Axler

4th edition, 2024

This proof-oriented text develops finite-dimensional linear algebra through vector spaces, linear maps, eigenvalues, inner-product spaces, and operator structure. It is valuable for advanced students needing conceptual foundations for information theory and theoretical computer science.

Recommended

Matrix Analysis

Roger A. Horn and Charles R. Johnson

2nd edition, 2012

A graduate-level reference on eigenvalue inequalities, canonical forms, matrix norms, positive definite matrices, and singular-value methods. Its depth is especially useful in signal processing, coding theory, optimization, and communication-system research.

Recommended

Matrix Analysis and Applied Linear Algebra

Carl D. Meyer

2nd edition, 2023

Meyer combines rigorous matrix analysis with computational techniques and applications from scientific computing, probability, networks, and engineering. It bridges abstract linear algebra and practical matrix-based modeling.

Recommended

Probability and Random Processes

Probability, Random Variables, and Stochastic Processes

Athanasios Papoulis and S. Unnikrishna Pillai

4th edition, 2002

This engineering-oriented classic develops probability, random variables, stochastic processes, spectral analysis, and estimation. It remains highly relevant to communication engineering, signal processing, detection theory, and random-signal research.

Recommended

Probability and Random Processes

Geoffrey Grimmett and David Stirzaker

3rd edition, 2001

A mathematically mature treatment of probability, random walks, branching processes, Markov chains, martingales, queues, and continuous-time processes. It provides strong preparation for information theory, stochastic modeling, and theoretical computer science.

Recommended

Introduction to Probability Models

Sheldon M. Ross

13th edition, 2023

Ross develops probabilistic modeling through Markov chains, Poisson processes, renewal theory, queues, reliability, Brownian motion, martingales, and simulation. Its applications make it useful across engineering, computer science, and operations research.

Recommended

Discrete Mathematics

Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications

Kenneth H. Rosen

8th edition, 2019

This widely adopted textbook covers logic, proofs, sets, relations, counting, recurrences, number theory, graphs, trees, Boolean algebra, and algorithms. It provides a broad foundation for computer science, coding theory, cryptography, and combinatorics.

Recommended

Concrete Mathematics: A Foundation for Computer Science

Ronald L. Graham, Donald E. Knuth, and Oren Patashnik

2nd edition, 1994

This seminal text develops continuous and discrete tools for rigorous algorithm analysis, including sums, recurrences, number theory, generating functions, and asymptotics. Its problem-solving style is invaluable in algorithms and combinatorics.

Recommended

Discrete Mathematics: Elementary and Beyond

László Lovász, József Pelikán, and Katalin Vesztergombi

1st edition, 2003

This engaging text develops combinatorics, number theory, graph theory, probability, and discrete structures through carefully chosen problems and proofs. It suits mathematically strong students preparing for theoretical computer science or combinatorial optimization.

Recommended

Abstract Algebra and Finite Fields

Abstract Algebra

David S. Dummit and Richard M. Foote

3rd edition, 2004

A comprehensive graduate treatment of groups, rings, modules, fields, Galois theory, and related algebraic structures. Its breadth makes it a standard reference for coding theory, cryptography, finite fields, and theoretical mathematics.

Recommended

Finite Fields

Rudolf Lidl and Harald Niederreiter

2nd edition, 1997

This authoritative monograph develops the algebraic structure, polynomials, equations, exponential sums, sequences, and applications of finite fields. It is foundational for error-correcting codes, cryptography, combinatorial design, and pseudorandom sequences.

Recommended

Fundamentals of Error-Correcting Codes

W. Cary Huffman and Vera Pless

1st edition, 2003

Huffman and Pless present coding theory from algebraic and engineering perspectives, covering linear, cyclic, BCH, Reed–Solomon, and self-dual codes with decoding methods. It connects abstract algebra with reliable communication and storage.

Recommended

Information Theory

Elements of Information Theory

Thomas M. Cover and Joy A. Thomas

2nd edition, 2006

This standard text develops entropy, data compression, channel capacity, rate-distortion theory, hypothesis testing, and network information theory. It is essential reading in communication engineering, coding theory, machine learning, and theoretical computer science.

Recommended

Information Theory: Coding Theorems for Discrete Memoryless Systems

Imre Csiszár and János Körner

2nd edition, 2011

This rigorous classic develops coding theorems using the method of types and treats source coding, channel coding, multi-terminal systems, varying channels, and information-theoretic security. It provides a theorem-driven foundation for modern information theory.

Recommended

Information Theory and Network Coding

Raymond W. Yeung

1st edition, 2008

Yeung develops entropy and classical information theory before treating network coding and information inequalities systematically. The book is especially useful for communication networks, distributed storage, coding theory, and multi-user information systems.

Recommended

Optimization

Convex Optimization

Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe

1st edition, 2004

This landmark text presents convex sets, convex functions, duality, optimality conditions, approximation, and numerical algorithms with extensive engineering applications. It is a standard reference for signal processing, communications, control, and machine learning.

Recommended

Numerical Optimization

Jorge Nocedal and Stephen J. Wright

2nd edition, 2006

A rigorous treatment of line-search and trust-region methods, nonlinear least squares, constrained optimization, quadratic programming, and large-scale algorithms. It is essential for implementing optimization methods in scientific computing, communications, and machine learning.

Recommended

Nonlinear Programming

Dimitri P. Bertsekas

3rd edition, 2016

Bertsekas develops constrained and unconstrained nonlinear optimization, including convexity, duality, multiplier methods, and optimality conditions. The book supports advanced work in control, network resource allocation, communications, and data analysis.

Recommended

Graph Theory and Networks

Graph Theory

Reinhard Diestel

5th edition, 2017

Diestel gives a rigorous modern introduction to connectivity, matching, coloring, planarity, flows, extremal methods, infinite graphs, and structural results. It is a core reference in combinatorics, algorithms, network coding, and theoretical computer science.

Recommended

Introduction to Graph Theory

Douglas B. West

2nd edition, 2001

West develops graph theory through proofs, examples, and carefully graded exercises. Its coverage of trees, connectivity, matchings, coloring, planarity, extremal theory, and network flows supports advanced mathematics and computer science study.

Recommended

Networks

Mark Newman

2nd edition, 2018

Newman presents the mathematical and computational study of real-world networks, including random graphs, centrality, community structure, spreading, resilience, and dynamics. It is relevant to data science, communications, complex systems, and network analysis.

Recommended

Numerical Methods

Numerical Analysis

Richard L. Burden, J. Douglas Faires, and Annette M. Burden

10th edition, 2016

This established textbook covers numerical equations, interpolation, approximation, differentiation, integration, initial-value problems, boundary-value problems, and linear systems. It provides a broad foundation for scientific computing and engineering applications.

Recommended

Numerical Linear Algebra

Lloyd N. Trefethen and David Bau III

1st edition, 1997

A concise treatment of matrix factorizations, least squares, conditioning, stability, eigenvalue algorithms, and iterative methods. It is especially relevant to signal processing, optimization, data science, and large linear systems.

Recommended

Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms

Nicholas J. Higham

2nd edition, 2002

Higham rigorously analyzes rounding errors, floating-point arithmetic, conditioning, backward error, stability, and matrix-algorithm reliability. It helps researchers judge when numerical computations in engineering, optimization, and data analysis can be trusted.

Recommended

Statistical Mathematics

Statistical Inference

George Casella and Roger L. Berger

2nd edition, 2002

Casella and Berger develop probability models, estimation, sufficiency, decision theory, hypothesis testing, intervals, and asymptotic methods rigorously. It is a standard graduate text for statistics, machine-learning theory, and data-science research.

Recommended

Testing Statistical Hypotheses

Erich L. Lehmann, Joseph P. Romano, and Michael Wolf

4th edition, 2022

This advanced reference develops statistical testing through optimal tests, exponential families, unbiasedness, invariance, multiple testing, asymptotic theory, and resampling. It provides a rigorous foundation for modern inferential methodology.

Recommended

All of Statistics: A Concise Course in Statistical Inference

Larry Wasserman

1st edition, 2004

Wasserman surveys probability, estimation, testing, regression, classification, Bayesian inference, graphical models, nonparametric methods, and statistical learning. It gives computer-science and data-science students broad statistical preparation.

Recommended

Mathematical Foundations

Principles of Mathematical Analysis

Walter Rudin

3rd edition, 1976

Rudin develops rigorous analysis through metric spaces, sequences, continuity, differentiation, integration, function sequences, and multivariable analysis. Its concise proofs and demanding exercises prepare readers for probability, optimization, and theoretical research.

Recommended

Understanding Analysis

Stephen Abbott

2nd edition, 2015

Abbott introduces rigorous real analysis while emphasizing motivation, conceptual understanding, and the transition to proof-based mathematics. It suits advanced students preparing for analysis, probability, optimization, or theoretical computer science.

Recommended

How to Prove It: A Structured Approach

Daniel J. Velleman

3rd edition, 2019

Velleman teaches mathematical logic, set language, relations, functions, induction, and rigorous proof construction systematically. It provides essential preparation for abstract algebra, discrete mathematics, algorithms, and theoretical computer science.
