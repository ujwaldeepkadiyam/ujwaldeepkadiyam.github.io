Machine Learning

Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning

Christopher M. Bishop

1st edition, 2006

Bishop presents machine learning through probability, Bayesian methods, graphical models, kernels, neural networks, and approximate inference. Its mathematical depth makes it a standard graduate text for machine learning, pattern recognition, signal processing, and data science.

Recommended

Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction

Kevin P. Murphy

1st edition, 2022

Murphy develops modern machine learning using probability, decision theory, linear algebra, optimization, and computation. It covers supervised and unsupervised learning, neural networks, graphical models, Bayesian inference, and reinforcement learning.

Recommended

Machine Learning for Engineers

Osvaldo Simeone

1st edition, 2022

Simeone gives a self-contained introduction grounded in probability, information theory, inference, and optimization. Its engineering orientation is especially relevant to communication systems, signal processing, coding, distributed learning, and data-driven design.

Recommended

Deep Learning

Deep Learning

Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville

1st edition, 2016

This seminal text develops the mathematical foundations, optimization, architectures, regularization, and research directions underlying deep learning. It remains essential for representation learning, generative modeling, computer vision, natural-language processing, and modern AI.

Recommended

Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts

Christopher M. Bishop and Hugh Bishop

1st edition, 2024

Bishop and Bishop explain contemporary deep learning through probability, neural networks, gradient optimization, transformers, graph neural networks, latent-variable models, and generative methods. The text combines conceptual clarity with mathematical substance.

Recommended

Dive into Deep Learning

Aston Zhang, Zachary C. Lipton, Mu Li, and Alexander J. Smola

1st edition, 2024

This modern textbook combines mathematical explanations with executable implementations of neural networks, convolutional models, recurrence, attention, transformers, and optimization. Its notebook-based approach connects theory, experimentation, and practical model development.

Recommended

Agentic AI

AI Agents in Action

Micheal Lanham

2nd edition, June 2026

Lanham provides a practical treatment of LLM agents, tools, memory, planning, multi-agent coordination, Model Context Protocol, evaluation, and deployment. It is useful for practitioners engineering contemporary autonomous workflows.

Recommended

Building Applications with AI Agents

Michael Albada

1st edition, September 2025

Albada examines single-agent and multi-agent applications built around foundation models. The book covers tools, memory, orchestration, interfaces, evaluation, scalability, reliability, security, and deployment for applied study and research.

Recommended

An Introduction to MultiAgent Systems

Michael Wooldridge

2nd edition, 2009

Wooldridge provides theoretical foundations for intelligent agents and multi-agent systems, including reasoning, communication, cooperation, negotiation, coordination, and social choice. It remains a seminal academic reference beneath modern LLM-agent systems.

Recommended
