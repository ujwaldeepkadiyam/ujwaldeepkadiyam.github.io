Foundations of Statistics

Course Introduction

Welcome to Foundations of Statistics for Data Science! This course is designed to help you build a solid understanding of the fundamental concepts that power data-driven decision making. Whether you’re analyzing business performance, conducting research, or building machine learning models, a strong grasp of statistics is essential to make sense of data and uncertainty.

We begin with the basics of statistical thinking — understanding data types, measures of central tendency, and dispersion. You’ll learn how to summarize and visualize data using descriptive statistics and identify key patterns that reveal hidden insights.

Next, we’ll explore Probability Theory — the mathematical framework for reasoning under uncertainty. From fundamental axioms and laws to the Law of Total Probability and Bayes’ Theorem, you’ll gain the tools to model and interpret real-world randomness with clarity and confidence.

We’ll then move into Probability Distributions, where you’ll understand how data behaves in nature. You’ll study discrete and continuous distributions such as the Binomial, Poisson, Normal, and others — the building blocks of statistical inference.

Finally, the course culminates with Hypothesis Testing, where you’ll learn to draw conclusions about populations using sample data. You’ll explore test statistics, p-values, confidence intervals, and decision-making under uncertainty — all using hands-on examples and intuitive explanations.

By the end of this course, you’ll not only understand statistical theory but also gain the confidence to apply these concepts using practical examples — the perfect foundation for advanced analytics, machine learning, and data-driven research.
