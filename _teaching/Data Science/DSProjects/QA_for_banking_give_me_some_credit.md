# # Questions & Answers

# Here are the questions submitted by students and their answers.  

---

### Q1: What is PCA?
**Answer:** PCA (Principal Component Analysis) reduces the number of dimensions in a dataset by projecting it onto principal components that maximize variance.

---

### Q2: What is R-squared in ML?
**Answer:** R-squared measures how well the independent variables explain the variance of the dependent variable. A higher value indicates a better fit.
