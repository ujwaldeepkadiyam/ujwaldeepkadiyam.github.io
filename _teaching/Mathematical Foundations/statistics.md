<!-- #### Course Introduction  -->
<h4 style="color: #cc33cc;">Course Introduction</h4>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Welcome to <strong>Foundations of Statistics for Data Science</strong>!  
  This course is designed to help you build a solid understanding of the fundamental concepts that power data-driven decision making. Whether you’re analyzing business performance, conducting research, or building machine learning models, a strong grasp of statistics is essential to make sense of data and uncertainty.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  We begin with the <strong>basics of statistical thinking</strong> — understanding data types, measures of central tendency, and dispersion. You’ll learn how to summarize and visualize data using descriptive statistics and identify key patterns that reveal hidden insights. 
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Next, we’ll explore <strong>Probability Theory</strong> — the mathematical framework for reasoning under uncertainty. From fundamental <strong>axioms and laws</strong> to the <strong>Law of Total Probability</strong> and <strong>Bayes’ Theorem</strong>, you’ll gain the tools to model and interpret real-world randomness with clarity and confidence.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  We’ll then move into <strong>Probability Distributions</strong>, where you’ll understand how data behaves in nature. You’ll study discrete and continuous distributions such as the Binomial, Poisson, Normal, and others — the building blocks of statistical inference.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Finally, the course culminates with <strong>Hypothesis Testing</strong>, where you’ll learn to draw conclusions about populations using sample data. You’ll explore test statistics, p-values, confidence intervals, and decision-making under uncertainty — all using hands-on examples and intuitive explanations.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  By the end of this course, you’ll not only understand statistical theory but also gain the confidence to apply these concepts using practical examples — the perfect foundation for advanced analytics, machine learning, and data-driven research.
</p>
